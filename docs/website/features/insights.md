# Insights

**Included in Free.** KnowX has no paid version.

Open **KnowX**, then **Insights**. It answers two questions: what are people looking for, and which articles let them down.

![Insights with counts, a search chart and the least helpful articles](../images/insights.webp)

## At the top

- **Articles**: how many published help articles you have
- **Searches this week**
- **Searches with no results**
- **Helpful rate**: the share of Yes votes across all articles

plus a chart of searches over recent days.

## The lists

| List | What it tells you | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Top searches | The words people search most | Make sure each has a good article |
| Searches with no results | What people wanted and did not find in the last 30 days, with how many times | Write an article, or add the word to an existing one. A term leaves the list 30 days after its last empty search |
| Least helpful articles | Articles with more No than Yes votes, by name | Rewrite them |
| Recent comments | What readers wrote after a No | Read for the missing detail |

Each list has a short empty state when there is nothing yet.

## Dashboard card

The EmDash dashboard shows a KnowX card with empty searches this week and the least helpful article, with a link to **Open Insights**.
