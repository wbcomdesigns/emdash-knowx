# Your first help articles

**Included in Free.** KnowX has no paid version.

## 1. Make your topics

In admin, open **Help categories** and add a few topics, for example **Getting started**, **Using the product** and **Account and billing**. Give each a short description: it shows on the topic card.

A topic can sit under another one. If your site helps with several products, make each product a top-level category and put its topics under it. KnowX then shows the products as cards on the help home, each with the icon you pick in **Settings**, **Product icons**.

## 2. Write an article

1. Open **Help articles** and choose **Add New**.
2. Write a **Title** and the **Content**. Use headings: each one appears in the article's table of contents.
3. Add a short **Excerpt**. It shows under the title in lists and search results.
4. Optionally fill **Applies to version** (for example `2.4`). The article shows it as a badge.
5. Pick a **Help category** and publish.

## 3. Add a few FAQs

Open **FAQs**, add a **Question** and an **Answer**, and publish. They appear on `/docs/faq/` sorted by question, A to Z.

## 4. Look at it as a visitor

Open `/docs/` in a private window. Search for a word from your article, open it, and vote **Yes** or **No** at the bottom. A day later, **KnowX**, **Insights** shows your first searches and votes.
